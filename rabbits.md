# All About Rabbits 🐰

## Introduction
Rabbits are small mammals known for their long ears, powerful hind legs, and short fluffy tails. They belong to the family Leporidae, which includes both rabbits and hares.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Size**: Most rabbits are 20-40 cm long
- **Weight**: 1-4 kg (varies by breed)
- **Distinctive Features**:
  - Long ears
  - Powerful back legs
  - Short, fluffy tail
  - Large eyes with good peripheral vision

## Behavior
Rabbits are:
* Social animals that live in groups
* Most active at dawn and dusk (crepuscular)
* Known for "binky" jumps when happy
* Territorial, marking their space with scent glands

## Diet
Rabbits are herbivores and eat:
1. Grass
2. Hay
3. Leafy greens
4. Vegetables
5. Herbs

## Interesting Facts
- A male rabbit is called a buck
- A female rabbit is called a doe
- A baby rabbit is called a kit or kitten
- Rabbits can see behind themselves without turning their heads
- A rabbit's teeth never stop growing

## Popular Rabbit Breeds
| Breed | Size | Notable Features |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| Dutch | Small | Classic black and white pattern |
| Netherland Dwarf | Tiny | Very small with short ears |
| Flemish Giant | Large | Can grow to over 6kg |
| Rex | Medium | Plush velvet-like fur |

## Care Tips
> Rabbits make wonderful pets but require proper care and attention. Always ensure they have:
> - Fresh water daily
> - Unlimited hay
> - Regular veterinary check-ups
> - Safe space to exercise
> - Social interaction

---
*Remember: Rabbits can live 8-12 years with proper care. They're a long-term commitment!*
